Administrator app · Flutter

Run the team from a phone

Managers add employees, plan the week, hand out tasks and decide on requests without opening a laptop.

  • Dashboard with tasks today, active employees, hours this week and pending requests.
  • Employee list with annual leave balance and live shift status.
  • Daily tasks with priority and weekly schedules built from custom shift types.
  • Approve or reject vacation requests, with overtime logging per employee.
  • Salary structure with hourly rate, overtime rate, deductions, insurance and tax, plus monthly payroll.
  • Analytics for task completion, attendance rate, absenteeism and overtime.
Employee app · Flutter

Everything an employee needs for the shift

Employees see their shift, tick off tasks, ask for time off and swap shifts with colleagues.

  • Today's shift with next shift and one-tap start of shift.
  • Weekly schedule and task list with priority and status filters.
  • Vacation requests with approval status, and shift transfers with outgoing and incoming requests.
  • Attendance log that marks each day as present or late, plus overtime and payslips.
