Customer app · Flutter

Shop, order and follow every step

Shoppers browse by category or search by product or brand, check out with cash on delivery, and follow the order until it arrives.

  • Guest browsing, with sign-in by email, phone number or Google.
  • Category browsing, featured and best-selling products, and search by product or brand.
  • Cart with promo codes, saved delivery addresses and cash-on-delivery checkout.
  • Order tracking from pending to delivered, with ratings for the product, the delivery and the service.
  • Refund requests for a whole order or selected items, plus downloadable invoices.
Admin panel · Django

Manage the catalogue, orders and shipping

The team keeps products, orders, shipments, refunds and customer messages in one place, with analytics on sales and traffic.

  • Dashboard with orders today, revenue and top-selling products.
  • Products with brands, SKU, stock, tax, discounts and descriptions, plus categories and subcategories in several languages.
  • Orders, shipments with shipping companies and tracking numbers, refunds, regions and delivery fees.
  • Support tickets, push notifications to customers, and app settings.
  • Analytics for visitors, page views, fulfilment rate, conversion, revenue, profit and revenue per product.
Customer website · Django

The same store in the browser

The website offers the full shopping flow, from browsing to order tracking and reviews, for customers who prefer a desktop.
